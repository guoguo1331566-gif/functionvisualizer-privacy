Quick tips

  • 2D: type expressions like sin(x), x^2, 1/x.
  • 3D: plot surfaces like x^2+y^2 and implicit equations.
  • Calculus: ∫(x^2,0,1), d(sin(x),0), ∫(sin(t),0,x).
  • Export: high-resolution PNG images, or data as CSV.

Function Visualizer works fully offline and collects no data. See the Privacy Policy.
